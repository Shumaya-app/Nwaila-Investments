Privacy notice

What we do with your information

Short version: the health check runs entirely in your browser and we never see it unless you choose to send it to us. Last updated 5 September 2026.

The business health check

Your answers are held in your browser and nowhere else. There is no account, no database and no cookie storing them. Your results link contains the answers inside the web address itself, which is why the link works on any device and why we have no copy of it.

Anyone you share that link with can see your report. Treat it the way you would treat any document about your business.

Requesting a discovery call

When you ask for a call, you send us your name, email address, business name and, if you completed it, your health check results. We use that to prepare for the conversation and to reply to you. The form is hosted by Tally, and submissions reach us by email at info@nwaila.co.za.

We keep enquiries for as long as we are in contact and for a reasonable period afterwards. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone outside Nwaila Investments unless you ask us to.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts of the site people use. It records things like which pages were viewed and roughly where in the world the visit came from, with IP addresses shortened before storage. It does not tell us who you are. Your browser's do-not-track setting or any ad blocker will stop it.

Your rights under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act you may ask us to:

  • tell you what information about you we hold;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete what you have sent us;
  • stop contacting you.

Email info@nwaila.co.za and we will act on it. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
